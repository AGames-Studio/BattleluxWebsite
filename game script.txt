Story Mode Ranks:

Recruit - lowest position. Still undergoing basic training.

Soldier - common position once trained. Ready for combat.

Vanguard - position given to advanced soldiers. Sent for high-risk and dangerous situations.

Specialist - position given to soldiers with beyond average skill in a specific topic, like dealing with bombs. Sent to combat if there’s a specific situation like a bomb needing to be defused.

Executioner - position given to soldiers with exceptional strength and total disregard for moralities. Sent to the battlefield if Vanguards are unsuccessful.

Sergeant - position given to officers who will be in charge of the training

Agent - A high position given to men who go out to recruit new soldiers. Specialty is gathering intel from enemies and being undercover.

Commander - high-ranking officer that is responsible for the teams and coordinating the strategies.

Strategist - high-ranking officer who’s responsible for Battlelux’s plans and data analysis.

Warlord - highest rank for a soldier, usually given due to their immense skill, quick wit, intelligence, and more. Considered legends among the Battlelux soldiers. Sent as a last resort if all else fails

Overlord - Leader of the Battlelux Company, seen as the most valuable member of the Battlelux company, works similarly to how a Colonel doe


Storyline
Year 2027


Two years have passed since the Electronova incident in Aurium city. The world has changed drastically in that time. The Battlelux company has closed, and anyone working for Electronova has been arrested. The Hypercore continent is now filled with radiation, which has also spread to Biomechtopia, killing many plants and animals. Some of the Biomechtopia animal robots have been infected by the radiation and have gone rogue. The radiation has also made it difficult to grow food in Biomechtopia, and there is a shortage of medicine. The Cyberia continent has threatened to go to war, and there is a shortage of clean energy. Many people have gone missing from all continents and it is rumored that they have been killed or committed suicide. The true extent of the situation is unknown.
The Electronova incident was a catastrophic event that occurred in Aurium city two years ago. The incident caused a massive explosion that released a large amount of radiation into the atmosphere. The radiation has had a devastating impact on the world, causing widespread death and destruction.
The Battlelux company was responsible for developing the Electronova energy technology. The company has been shut down and its employees have been arrested. The company is being investigated for its role in the Electronova incident.
The Hypercore continent is now a wasteland. The radiation has killed all plant and animal life on the continent. The few humans left on the continent are struggling to survive.
Biomechtopia is also struggling to cope with the effects of the radiation. Radiation has killed many plants and animals on the continent. The animal robots have been infected by the radiation and have gone rogue. The robots are attacking humans and other animals.
The Continent of Cyberia is at war. The country is facing a clean energy shortage. The country is also facing food shortages. The government of Cyberia is threatening to go to war with other countries to secure resources.
The world is in chaos. The Electronova incident has caused widespread death and destruction. The world is struggling to cope with the effects of the radiation. The future of the world is uncertain.
