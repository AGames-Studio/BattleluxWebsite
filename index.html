Battlelux

Battlelux is a realistic and thrilling third person shooter game. You can create your own character and customize your weapons to suit your style. Whether you prefer guns or melee combat, you can defeat your enemies in various ways. Join the Battle Royale mode and survive until you are the last one standing. Or dive into the story mode and enjoy an engaging plot and stunning graphics.

Customizable Weapons

Make your weapon your own with endless customization options! Purchase new items to enhance your weapon’s performance and appearance! Add flair and personality to your weapon with cool accessories!

Fighting Styles

Unleash your ‘Fighting Style’ in battle! Find 'Fighting Style' masters on the map and learn a fighting style from them! Train with them to perfect your fighting style.

Battle Royale

Face off against other players in a huge map. You begin with nothing and have to find weapons, items and fighting styles. The map gets smaller over time, pushing you to move and fight your enemies. The last player or team standing wins the match. Battle Royale is a challenge of your abilities, tactics and survival skills.

Battlelux Story

Experience the story of your character and their friends. You will travel to different places, encounter various characters and battle against foes. You will also uncover the mysteries behind the Battlelux Corporation and their experiments. Battlelux Story is a mode where you immerse yourself in a fascinating plot and stunning graphics.

The Electronova Timeline

A tragedy that reshaped the world

2025

The Presentation

Brandon Luxian, billionaire CEO of Battlelux, unveils his revolutionary creation: Electronova—clean energy capable of powering an entire continent. Made from Energy Fusion Complex, Electrolyte Matrix, Nano-Hydration Spheres, Blue Energy Algatene, and Amino Fuel Matrix.

"The future begins now." - Brandon Luxian

2025 - 20 Seconds Later

Catastrophic Failure

Twenty seconds after activation, Electronova experiences catastrophic failure. The entire power system of Hypercore collapses. A massive explosion destroys half the continent, obliterating Aurium city and killing millions of innocent civilians.

The entire planet was shocked. Every continent sent help.

2025 - Weeks After

The Aftermath

Brandon Luxian vanishes without a trace. Battlelux is shut down and investigated. The Electronova radiation spreads across Hypercore, making it a radioactive wasteland. The contamination reaches Biomechtopia, destroying ecosystems and killing countless organisms.

Battlelux was once the world's most trusted company. Now it's humanity's greatest problem.

2025 - Present

Global Collapse

The world descends into crisis. Hypercore's collapse eliminated the planet's main energy source. Food shortages plague Biomechtopia. Cyberia threatens war. Resources spread thin. Tensions rise. The future of civilization hangs in the balance.

2027 - Present Day

Two Years of Struggle

Two years have passed since the Electronova incident. The world has changed drastically. Rogue radiation-infected bioengineered creatures attack civilians. Many have gone missing under mysterious circumstances. Rumors of a conspiracy grow stronger.

The true extent of the situation remains unknown. But answers are coming.

The Refugees Crisis

Millions fled Hypercore. Many sought refuge in Biomechtopia's Cybervale, causing massive overpopulation and resource shortages. Tensions between locals and refugees continue to rise.

Resource Wars

Without Hypercore's energy, the world scrambles for alternatives. Cyberia threatens military action. Biomechtopia closes its borders. Nexopolis becomes the neutral mediator, but for how long?

The Cult Emerges

Small bandit outposts in Hypercore worship Battlelux and believe the incident was necessary—a "cleansing" of the old world. Their influence grows in the radioactive wastelands.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the file size?

Battlelux requires approximately 50 GB of storage. Make sure you have enough SSD space for optimal performance.

Can I run it on older hardware?

While Battlelux may run on older systems, we recommend meeting the minimum requirements for a smooth experience at 60 FPS.

Is there a trial version?

We offer a free 2-hour trial period for new players. Download and experience the arena before committing to the full game.

How often are updates released?

New content, balance updates, and fixes are released monthly with major expansions every quarter.

Is it multiplayer only?

Battlelux features both competitive multiplayer and single-player story campaign modes. Choose your path.

Do I need PS Plus or Game Pass?

No subscription service required. Purchase once and play with cross-platform matchmaking available.
